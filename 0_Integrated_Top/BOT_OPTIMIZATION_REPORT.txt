Vivado can attribute optimized combinational logic to different hierarchy
boundaries and combine LUTs across boundaries. Parent/child counts therefore
must not be summed to reconstruct the top-level Slice LUT count. The overall
report is the acceptance criterion. In particular, additional market logic
was attributed to the top level in the original baseline. The unchanged
bot ledger's small LUT difference is a mapping effect, not a source change.

OPTIONS CONSIDERED BEFORE IMPLEMENTATION
These were engineering estimates, not claimed measurements. All estimates
are relative to the original full bot-enabled design.

1. Share player/bot ledger and risk arithmetic using an arbiter.
   Estimated saving: 500-900 LUTs, 300-600 FFs, 4-7 DSPs; no extra BRAM.
   Latency: tens to hundreds of additional cycles while requests serialize.
   Risks: changes player-order backpressure, fill handshakes and snapshot
   ownership; more invasive verification. Can preserve behavior if designed
   carefully, but is unnecessary given the measured result below.

2. Replace bot ledger/risk with a compact fixed-lot accountant.
   Estimated saving: 400-700 LUTs, 250-500 FFs, 3-5 DSPs; no extra BRAM.
   Latency: roughly 5-20 accounting cycles per update.
   The current bot is flat or holds one 20-share lot, so it never needs the
   general ledger's partial-sale division. Its cost basis, fees, cash and
   per-stock unrealized P&L could be kept in a compact FSM.
   Risks: dedicated accounting logic must be proven against ledger.v;
   future variable-lot/partial-sale behavior would need further support.

3. Single-write history RAM plus mathematically bounded market arithmetic.
   Estimated saving: 1,500-3,000 LUTs, approximately 500 FFs, 0-4 DSPs;
   no extra BRAM. Measured overall saving is larger: 8,288 LUTs / 484 FFs /
   4 DSPs, including synthesis's changed arithmetic sharing and LUT mapping.
   Latency: 32 extra clocks (0.32 us at 100 MHz) only on the first tick;
   subsequent bot ticks and all market output latencies are unchanged.
   Risks: RAM must not be read before initialization; narrowed signed
   arithmetic must preserve rounding, clamp order and saturation.
   SELECTED: retains both existing ledgers and the existing risk datapaths.

Reusing the market history was also inspected. Its published interfaces
expose 7 days and bucketed 30/180-day histories, not the exact bot-padded
8-day window. Adding an 8-day interface would couple startup padding to
the market and require extra handling. A 512-bit local LUTRAM is simpler.

WHY THE IMPLEMENTATION SAVES HARDWARE
1. bot_trader.v previously assigned eight history addresses in one clock
   during startup. Vivado could not infer RAM and used 512 history FFs plus
   large read/write selection logic. INIT now writes one slot per clock.
   There is one unreset memory write process and one asynchronous read.
   Synthesis confirms hist_reg is 32 x 16 distributed RAM (RAM32M x 3).
   All eight padded copies exist before that stock's first decision.
   The bot trader itself drops from 648 FFs to 133 and 959 LUTs to 272.

2. stock.v used signed 32-bit temporaries and a signed 64-bit division for
   each stock's combinational price update. Explicit bounds are:
     random plus bias: -10..285, signed 10 bits;
     clamped random: 0..100; offset: -50..50;
     volatility, clamped before narrowing: 0..100;
     weighted offset: -5000..5000, signed 14 bits;
     daily percent: -100..100, signed 8 bits;
     multiplier: 0..200, unsigned 8 bits;
     scaled price: at most 65535*200 = 13,107,000 < 2^24.
   Signed /50 retains truncation toward zero. Since scaled price is always
   nonnegative, unsigned 24-bit /100 gives the identical integer result.
   The 1..65534 output saturation is unchanged. No reciprocal approximation
   or arithmetic synthesis directive is used. One DSP per stock is saved.

BOT BEHAVIOR AND VIEWS
ENABLE_BOT defaults to 1 in Integration_Top and C_portfolio_top. The direct
synthesis script also explicitly supplies ENABLE_BOT=1. bot_active follows
that parameter, so the normal board build has active bot hardware and UI.
All four stocks retain their exact padded 8-day windows, MA>>8 dead band,
20-share buy when flat, full-position sell, spread, fees, affordability
checks, one fill per stock per day and ledger busy handshake.
The player ledger, bot ledger, order book and game FSM are preserved.
The existing non-displayed return/drawdown dividers remain disabled as they
were in the measured starting tree; no additional metric was disabled here.
Real MA-band signals now feed the right OLED instead of constant zeros.
The trade log captures actual fill pulses, rather than detecting changes
in last-trade fields. Left bot-view row 6 now shows live cash using the shared
BCD fields. Right bot view retains equity, holdings and per-stock P&L.
There is no strategy compromise. The startup latency above is the only
intentional change to the trading schedule.
